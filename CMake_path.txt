/CMakeLists.txt is started.
/vcpkg.json is processed to learn which final packages are needed 
(boost-thread, ms-gsl, openssl, opus, protobuf).
The toolchain you provide (either with CMakePresets.json) or manually with 
CMake) is consulted for the totality of packages needed for download.
/cmake/setup_dependencies.cmake is included and processed. This sets up 
the /_dependencies directory, and downloads GSL, Quickpool, wepoll and 
cmake_compiler_flags.
/cmake/compiler_utilities.cmake is included and processed.
/src/CMakeLists.txt should be included and processed next.
If LIBMUMBLE_BUILD_EXAMPLES set, /examples/CMakeLists.txt should be included 
and processed next.  Downloads toml11 (in _dependencies) and compiles libraries
for /examples/ExampleClient and /examples/ExampleServer
